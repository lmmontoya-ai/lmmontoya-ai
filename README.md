# Luis M. Montoya

**Empirical AI safety researcher working on scalable oversight of LLM agents beyond self-report.**

Pivotal Research Fellow (extension program) · M.S. in Computer Science (ML), Georgia Tech (expected 2027) · B.S. in Physics, Universidad EIA

I study how to keep LLM agents legible and monitorable when what they say and reason cannot be taken at face value, specifically, whether their behavior can be forecast, their unverbalized representations detected, and their misconduct surfaced by peers or their environment. My work measures how much reliable information about an agent's context, goals, and future actions is recoverable from its behavior, its internal activations, and peer observations, especially when verbal reports are absent or unfaithful.

Previously MATS 9.0 exploration phase in Neel Nanda's mechanistic interpretability stream. I have built production ML since 2022, including fraud and risk detection at Mercado Libre; currently Senior Data Scientist (contractor) at Vantora alongside research.

### Selected work

1. **[Towards Measuring and Detecting Unverbalized Evaluation Awareness](https://github.com/lmmontoya-ai/Non-verbal-Eval-Awareness)** - Co-first author, ICML 2026 Mechanistic Interpretability Workshop. Evaluation-conditioned behavior persists (+7.0pp and +65.3pp across two model organisms) after verbalized evaluation awareness is suppressed to an audited 0%, while activation probes still recover the eval/deploy context. [Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=zGmgeR0f4D) · w/ Reilly Haskins and Benjamin Adams.

2. **[When Can Language Models Predict Each Other?](https://luismmontoya.dev/r/self-other-prediction-sep26/)** - Sole fellow, Pivotal Research Fellowship (mentors: Damiano Fornasiere and Gaël Gendron, LawZero). Across three interactive settings, a simple situation-plus-action-count baseline matched or beat model action forecasters, including in new-situation transfer tests. Selected for the extension program. Contracting environment: [ledger](https://github.com/lmmontoya-ai/ledger).

3. **[Peer-Reporting Benchmark](https://github.com/lmmontoya-ai/peer-reporting-benchmark)** — Current work. Does an observer agent privately report a peer's evidence-supported approval breach? Data collection is complete, with 216 assignments in the main study and a separate extension scaling to 100 scripted peers.

4. **[GLM-5.3-Flash User Conditioning](https://github.com/lmmontoya-ai/glm53-user-conditioning)** — Sole author. A preregistered confirmatory replication (34,400 rows) of Transluce's user-awareness task, finding that models state slightly lower confidence to famous AI figures than generic fame predicts (−0.65pp, 95% CI [−1.21, −0.10]).

5. **[Warm-Starting TTT-E2E](https://github.com/lmmontoya-ai/Warm-starting-TTT-E2E)** — First author, manuscript in preparation (Georgia Tech, advised by Prof. Vijay K. Madisetti). Warm-starting long-context test-time training from pretrained transformers is ~7.4× cheaper than training from scratch, which retains the final-quality lead at 125M and 760M scales.
